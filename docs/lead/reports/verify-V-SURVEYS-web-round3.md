### HIGH

**N6. One person's answers, and one person's SKIP, can reach the person who signed in later in the same browser (stale tab).**
- Where: `src/features/surveys/SurveyScreen.tsx:75-81` (`given` lives in React state), `:179-214` (`submit` posts), `:114-121` (`sendSkip`); the token is read when the request goes out, `src/lib/api/client.ts:166` (`sessionStorage.getToken()`, which is `localStorage`, shared by all tabs). The round 3 fix keys only the stored draft by person (`logic.ts:50`); the page itself never remembers whose answers it holds.
- What I did (1440x900, one browser context, two tabs): tab 1: A signs in, opens a three question survey, answers Q1 (choice) and Q2 (recorded voice), picks a choice on Q3. Tab 2: Settings, Sign out, sign in as B. Tab 1: "Send answer".
- What I saw: `userB POST /surveys/s3/responses {"answers":[{"questionId":"q1","optionId":"h1"},{"questionId":"v2","clipId":"c240"},{"questionId":"q2","optionId":"o2"}]}` (A's two choices and A's clip id under B's token) and tab 1 showed "Thanks. Your answers are in." with A's three answers (`N2-stale-tab-after-B-signs-in-1440.png`). Second run, same set-up but B never opens the survey and tab 1 presses "Leave": the stub got `userA START` then `userB SKIP` (B's skip count goes up for a survey B never saw; two of those lock B out).
- Should happen: the page keeps the token subject it started with; before it sends an answer, a SKIP or a draft save it compares that with the current one, and if they differ it sends nothing, clears and shows Question 1 or "Sign in again". (Narrow in likelihood, needs two tabs and a shared device, but it is exactly "A's answers reach B" and "B locked out by A".)
