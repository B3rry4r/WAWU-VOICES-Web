### MEDIUM

**M-A. A hidden free answer's count can be read off the released report.** `api/src/surveys/survey-report.ts:140-177` and `surveys-results.service.ts:404-411`. The report shows `answered` (all answers including hidden ones) and the list of `texts` (without hidden ones).
- Did: 30 people, 29 write a text answer; staff hide 2; release.
- Happened: question 2 `answered: 29`, `texts` has 27 entries, so 2 were hidden. The hidden text is not in the report (checked), the count is.
- Should (the lead's ruling "its count is not derivable"): show `answered` for a free-text question as the number left in the list, or add the hidden count to nothing at all. Same for a voice answer without a transcript, or a text scrubbed to nothing, which are also dropped from the list but counted in `answered`.

**M-B. A number under the floor can be worked out from `answered` against `totals.responses`: how many people skipped a question.** `survey-report.ts:136-196`. A response may leave out any question.
- Did: 30 people answer question 1; 29 answer question 2.
- Happened: `totals.responses 30`, question 2 `answered 29`: exactly 1 person skipped question 2. A group of 1, from two numbers the report shows. (With a question most people skipped, `answered` is under the floor and null: that case is safe.)
- Should: show `answered` only for questions where every response answered, or round the skipped number away (hide `answered` when `responses - answered` is under the floor and above 0).

**M-C. The review list's answer ids are time-ordered, so staff can read when each free answer was written and in what order.** `SurveyAnswer.id` is `uuid(7)`; `GET /admin/surveys/{id}/free-answers` and the `PATCH` route return it as `id`.
- Did: two people answer 1.5 s apart (texts "zzz first", then "aaa second"); read the review list.
- Happened: the list is alphabetical ("aaa second" first) but each `id` carries the creation time to the millisecond (decoded 21 ms after my own clock) and sorting by `id` gives answer order. The first check's design (alphabetical, "never in the order people answered") is undone for staff. Anyone who knows when a friend answered can match a text to a person.
- Should: give each review item a random handle (for example a hash of the answer id and a secret), not the answer's own id; or make answer ids random (`uuid(4)`) in a migration for new rows.

**M-D. Release works without the free answers ever being read.** `surveys-results.service.ts:386-443`. `release` checks the snapshot and the lock, nothing else. The lead ruling is that text reaches the brand "only after an admin has read them". Read only for the missing check (I released a survey with no free answers with no read, which shows no read is required; I did not release one with free answers unread, but nothing in the code stops it). The audit trail shows the read (`survey.free.read`) or its absence afterwards, nothing forces it.
- Should: refuse release while `hiddenCount`/`total` has not been fetched at least once by the releasing person (a `reviewedAt`), or say plainly this is a process rule.

**M-E. The scrub still lets through contact details, and a name or address typed in plain words always goes through.** `survey-rules.ts:36-73`. Ran about 65 examples. Removed: emails, "at … dot …" with spaces, `[at] [dot]`, `https://` and `www.`, bare domains in a list of 27 endings, `t.me/` and `wa.me/`, `@handle`, handles after insta/instagram/ig/twitter/x/tiktok/snap/snapchat, phone numbers with spaces, dots, dashes, `+234`, spelled in words (six or more words), 10 to 11 digit account or BVN numbers.
Passed through unchanged: `email chidi(at)gmail(dot)com` (no spaces), `chidi @ gmail . com`, `kemi@gmail。com` (ideographic dot), `chidi at gmail.com` (becomes "chidi at [removed]"), `telegram: chidi_o`, `fb: chidi.okafor`, `kemi.studio` and `chidi.example` (ending not in the list), `1.2.3.4`, `08O3I234567` (letter O and I), "chidi dot com", "Call me Chidi Okafor from Surulere", "I am Adaeze Nwosu of 12 Allen Avenue Ikeja", "ask for Mrs Bello at the shop in Wuse 2".
Acceptable? With a person reading every item before release (and M-D enforced), what is left is names, addresses and a few spelt-out contact details, which a person can see; the review list shows the scrubbed text, so staff sees what the brand would. Without a forced read it is not acceptable. A small widening (`(at)`/`(dot)` without spaces, `telegram|fb|facebook|snap` handles, any `word.word` that is not a known sentence ending) would still help.

**M-F. Existing routes now refuse requests they accepted on origin/main.** `vote/post-content.ts:43`, `poll.service.ts:504`, `proposal.service.ts:369`. The rule (a survey clip never becomes a public post) needed only `SURVEY_ANSWER` to be refused. The builder also required the route's own purpose.
- Did: same spec on `origin/main` (its own checkout) and on this tree: a READY clip of each purpose as a poll explanation and as a proposal.
- Happened: origin/main (7 purposes, `SURVEY_ANSWER` does not exist there): all 201 on both routes. This tree (8 purposes): explanation 201 for `ANSWER` and `VOTE_EXPLANATION` only, 422 for the other 6 including `SURVEY_ANSWER`; proposal 201 for `PROPOSAL` only, 422 for the other 7. A client that uploads a proposal clip with purpose `ANSWER`, or an explanation with `PROPOSAL`, worked before and breaks now. The first check's brief was to leave routes alone "for a request that uses none of the new things".
- Should: either accept the narrowing on purpose (the lead decides, and the app is checked to send the right purpose), or refuse only `SURVEY_ANSWER` and clips on a survey answer.

### LOW

- **L-A. Residual bound after complementary suppression.** `survey-report.ts:79-98`. With 50, 20 and 1 the report shows "50" and "Other 21": one of the two hidden groups has at most 1 person (21 minus the floor of 20); with 40 Lagos, 20 Kano, 1 Abuja: "Lagos 40" and "Other 21", same. No position can be named, but "one of these is 1 or 0" is learned whenever the hidden sum is under twice the floor. Fix: when the hidden sum is under twice the floor, hide one more group.
- **L-B. Inclusion and exclusion across breakdowns.** 60 people: 20 share nothing, 39 share state and age band, 1 shares state only. Report: `byState Lagos 40`, `byAgeBand 25-34: 39`. The group "state but no age band" is 1, from the two numbers. Fix: show a breakdown only if every other breakdown's shared total equals its own or differs by at least the floor.
- **L-C. A NUL byte (`\u0000`) gives 500 on every new text input.** Brand name, tagline, website, survey title, brand name, question, option, state, decision reason, and **a person's text answer** (all 500); the existing text-answer route answers 400. No data loss; the cause is an unhandled database error (`brands.service.ts:114`, all survey create/update and `respond`). Fix: reject NUL in the DTO.
- **L-D. What still ties a response to a person in the database**, none reachable through a route: `SurveyEvent` rows `START`, `VIEW`, `SKIP` with `userId` and `createdAt` (the START to the next response by time matched 3 of 3, a start only needs "once per survey", it could be stored without the person's id after the first); `SurveyAnswer.clipId` to `VoiceClip.ownerId` for voice answers (needed so the person can play and delete it); `SurveyResponse.createdAt` and the time-ordered ids of `SurveyResponse` and `SurveyAnswer` (see M-C); `respondentKey`, an HMAC of survey and person under the server's phone pepper: safe unless the pepper leaks, then every (survey, person) pair can be computed and tested. Brief says these are kept for the frequency cap and the skip rule; a leaked database dump plus clocks identifies who answered.
- **L-E. Scrub is quadratic on a long unbroken string.** Timed (`tsx`): 20,000 letters 0.7 s; 50,000 letters 4.9 s; 200,000 letters **80 s**; 200,000 digits **76 s**; 50,000 `a.` repeated 7.5 s. `a@a@a@` x 200,000 characters: 5 ms (linear). At the real input limit (500 characters, and transcripts of a 30 second clip) it is under 2 ms, so not reachable by a person today; any other caller with long text would block the process for the whole run. The slow cases are the email, domain and "at/dot" expressions (`[\w.+-]+` with no length bound). Fix: cap the length passed in (the 500 limit is there; apply it to transcripts) or bound the expression.
- **L-F. Zero-width space breaks the duplicate-name rule.** `Dupe​Brand` next to `Dupe Brand`: 201, slug `dupe-brand-2`. Spaces are removed, other invisible characters are not. Fix: strip `\p{Cf}` in the key.
- **L-G. `BRAND_MAIL_DAILY_MAX` set to an empty string means 0.** `mail.service.ts:313`: `Number("")` is 0, so an environment file with `BRAND_MAIL_DAILY_MAX=` blocks every brand mail ("budget") with no error, and the sweep keeps trying. `0`, `" "`, `""` all give 0; `abc`, `-1`, `NaN` give 50; `1.9` gives 1; `5` gives 5; `1e2` gives 100. Fix: treat empty as unset.
- **L-H. Reopening a survey that is over but not yet frozen.** A paused survey past its end date is "over", but the snapshot is taken by the first read or the ten-minute sweep. Before that, `PATCH endsOn` into the future and `RESUME` both answer 200 and the survey runs again. No data leaks (one snapshot only), but "over" depends on who looks first. Fix: refuse a date edit that moves a past end date, or take the snapshot in the edit.
- **L-I. The 101st answer is refused with 404 "We could not find that survey".** Target 3, six people answering at once: 3 stored (201), 3 refused (404), nothing lost in the database, no event; the snapshot holds exactly the 3. The person sees "not found" rather than "this survey has closed".
- **L-J. The same clip can answer two questions in one request.** `surveys-public.service.ts:388` counts clips already stored, not those used earlier in the same request: 201, one recording on two answers. No cross-person effect.
- **L-K. List and detail show live counts while the survey runs.** `GET /admin/surveys` and `/{id}`: `views`, `starts`, `completes`, `skips` and `responses` (25 for a survey with 25 answers) update with each answer. This is more than "only how many have answered" and lets an admin see the moment of each answer tick up. No content.
- **L-L. Brand page lists a survey that is over.** `brands.service.ts:193`: `status = APPROVED` with no end or snapshot check; read only (not run).
