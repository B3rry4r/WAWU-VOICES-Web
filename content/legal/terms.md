## Signing in

## Who may post
You may post only if you are 18 or older and have finished your profile. Reading, listening, liking and sharing are open to everyone, including people who have not signed in.

## Community rules
The Community rules are part of these Terms. In short: go after the idea, not the person; no threats, harassment or hate; keep private details private; tag news honestly; one person, one vote; adults only. We step in for harm, never for a point of view. You can read the full rules on the Rules tab.

## Your posts
You own what you say. By posting, you let VOICES store it, play it, show it to other people, turn a recording into a written transcript, and share it with a link or card when you or someone else shares it. You can delete your own posts. Do not post anything you do not have the right to share.

## Tokens
- Tokens are a count inside VOICES. You buy them with naira, and the app shows each pack and its price before you pay. You get exactly the tokens you pay for, with no bonus tokens.
- They have no cash value. They cannot be cashed out, and they cannot be sent to another person.
- Your first 5 posts are free (a voice up to 60 seconds or text up to 500 characters). A longer post is charged in full and does not use a free post. After the free posts, a post costs tokens.
- A text message costs 5 tokens for every 150 characters, in proportion to its length. A text can be up to 5,000 characters.
- A voice message costs 78 tokens for up to 30 seconds and 100 tokens for over 30 and up to 60 seconds. A voice message can be up to 60 seconds.
- You are charged when you send a message. A recording you cancel costs nothing.
- You can send up to 20 messages a day, answers and replies together, free ones included. The day ends at midnight in Lagos. Reading, listening, likes, shares and reactions do not count.
- Votes and reactions also cost tokens once you have used the free amount shown in the app for the day.
- The cost of each action is shown in the app before you confirm it, and it can change over time.
- Reading, listening, liking and sharing are always free.
- Posting result numbers is free and does not use one of your free posts.
- Spent tokens are not returned. See the Refunds tab.

## Fix my text
Fix my text is an optional tool on the Write screen. It corrects spelling, typos and grammar in your own text, and nothing else. You see the changes and choose to use them or keep your own text. A fix costs 5 tokens. You are charged only when a fix comes back, and once for each request. If a fix cannot be made, or there is nothing to fix, you are not charged. The AI is used for nothing else on the Write screen.

## Payments
Payments are made through Flutterwave. We confirm every payment with Flutterwave ourselves before we add tokens to your account. We never rely on your browser alone to say a payment worked.

## Suspensions and appeals
If a post breaks the Community rules, we may remove it, warn you, suspend your account or, for serious cases, ban it. A suspension without an end date lasts 7 days. We tell you why. A suspended or banned person can still appeal. An appeal is decided by someone who did not take the action.

## Blocking
When you block someone, you and that person are hidden from each other. You can still open their profile to unblock them.

## News tags
If you share news, you must pick a tag: Unverified News or Gossip. Only WAWU editors and admins can mark news as Verified News.

## Verified results
WAWU does not publish election or other results of its own. People post the numbers they see, such as the totals on a result sheet. Each post shows Verified if the WAWU team has checked it, or Not verified if it has not. Post only what is written on the sheet. A photo of a result sheet is seen by the WAWU team only.

## Adverts
Some adverts appear in VOICES. Every advert is labelled with the name of the brand that placed it. An advert is not a post and is not an opinion of WAWU.

## Deleting your account
You can delete your account in Settings. We keep it for 14 days in case you change your mind. After 14 days it is deleted, and any tokens you have not spent are lost. They are not paid out. Some records, such as votes, result totals and payment records, stay without your name on them. The Privacy tab explains this.
