## Voice Units
- You buy Voice Units with naira, through Flutterwave. The pack and its price are shown before you pay.
- Voice Units have no cash value. They are never cashed out and never sent to another person.
- Units do not expire at the moment. If that changes, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
- Units you have spent are not refunded. A post you delete does not return the units it cost, and does not return a free post.

## Extra voice tokens
- A token covers one longer message. The price is shown before you pay.
- An unused token stays on your account until you use it.
- A token that has been used is not refunded.
- If a recording fails before it is posted, the token is not used up.

## Payments that fail or are not confirmed
- If a payment fails or is not confirmed by Flutterwave, you are not charged, and no units or token are added.
- If your bank takes your money but the payment is not confirmed, we keep checking. A payment that is confirmed late is picked up within 72 hours, and the units or token then appear on your account.
- If it is still not confirmed after 72 hours, write to us and we will look at it by hand.
- Tapping Pay twice never makes two payments.

## Paying too much or too little
- If you pay more than the price, you get the units you bought, once. The difference is refunded to you by hand. We do not pay it out automatically, so it can take a little time.
- If you pay less than the price, the payment is refused, and no units are added.

## Deleting your account
Voice Units you have not spent when your account is deleted are lost, and are not refunded. We say this before you confirm.

## How to ask
Write to support@wawuafrica.com. Include the email on your WAWU ID, the date of the payment and the payment reference or receipt number from the app. If you think a payment was taken by mistake, tell us the same day if you can.
