## Tokens
- You buy tokens with naira, through Flutterwave. The pack, its price, the VAT, the payment charge and the total are shown before you pay. You get exactly the tokens you pay for.
- Tokens have no cash value. They are never cashed out and never sent to another person.
- Tokens do not expire at the moment. If that changes, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
- Tokens from a plan are added month by month. Once they are added, they are treated like any other tokens: spent tokens are not refunded, and tokens you keep do not expire.
- A plan is not refunded once it is paid, except where the law says it must be. It does not renew, so there is nothing to cancel.
- Tokens you have spent are not refunded. A post you delete does not return the tokens it cost, and does not return a free post.
- A message is charged only when it is sent. A recording you cancel costs nothing, and a message that fails to send is not charged.
- Fix my text is charged only when a fix comes back. If it fails, or there is nothing to fix, you are not charged.
- If you bought an extra voice token before tokens had these prices, it stays on your account until you use it, and covers one message.

## Payments that fail or are not confirmed
- If a payment fails or is not confirmed by Flutterwave, you are not charged, and no tokens are added.
- If your bank takes your money but the payment is not confirmed, we keep checking. A payment that is confirmed late is picked up within 72 hours, and the tokens then appear on your account.
- If it is still not confirmed after 72 hours, write to us and we will look at it by hand.
- Tapping Pay twice never makes two payments.

## Paying too much or too little
- If you pay more than the total shown, you get the tokens you bought, once. The difference is refunded to you by hand. We do not pay it out automatically, so it can take a little time.
- If you pay less than the total shown, the payment is refused, and no tokens are added.

## Deleting your account
Tokens you have not spent when your account is deleted are lost, and are not refunded. We say this before you confirm.

A plan that is running stops when you delete your account. The months that have not yet been added are not added.

## How to ask
Write to support@wawuafrica.com. Include the email on your WAWU ID, the date of the payment and the payment reference or receipt number from the app. If you think a payment was taken by mistake, tell us the same day if you can.
