## What we collect
- From your WAWU ID when you sign in: your WAWU ID number. The sign-in also carries your email address and phone number. We do not keep your email address in the VOICES database.
- Your phone number is never stored. We keep only a one-way code made from it. It cannot be turned back into your number. We use it so that one phone number has one account and one vote.
- What you choose when you join: your handle, your country, and whether you are 18 or older. We keep that confirmation and the time you accepted the Terms and the Community rules.
- What you add to your profile: a display name, photo, short bio, languages, state and city, and the topics you follow.
- What you post: voice recordings, the written transcript of each recording, text posts and replies, votes, reactions, likes and saves.
- Listen counts: we count how many times each voice is played and how many people listen to the end.
- We record which voices you play and how much of each, to rank answers. Nobody else sees this. It is deleted with your account; for visitors who are not signed in, 90 days after their last listen.
- Reports you file, and the decisions and appeals that follow.
- Videos: only when you ask for one, we make a short video of your own voice answer (the question, a moving waveform of your voice, captions from its transcript, and your name only if you chose to show it on your card). Only you can see it or save it. We do not publish it. If you delete the answer, or we remove it, the video is deleted too.
- Referral codes: you get a code of your own, and if you joined with someone’s code we keep who invited you.
- Invite links: you get a personal invite link. If you open someone’s link, then join and answer, we keep that they invited you. They see how many of their friends have answered and each friend’s handle. They see a friend’s word only if that friend keeps “Share my word” on. It is on unless you turn it off, in Settings or when you pick your word, and turning it off takes effect straight away. Nobody else sees it through this. An answer you post anonymously never counts and never shows to the person who invited you.
- Support messages: the text, the topic, any payment you point to, and the email you may add so we can reply. If you were signed in, the message is linked to your account.
- Payments: the status, the amount, the currency, our reference, the Flutterwave number for the payment, the amount charged, the date, and the card brand and last four digits. We never keep a full card number. Your email is sent to Flutterwave when you pay, because Flutterwave needs it. VOICES does not keep it in its own records.
- Events about how VOICES is used: opening a share link, and seeing or tapping an advert. Each is tied to your account if you are signed in, or to the guest id below if you are not.
- A guest id: if you have not signed in, your browser makes a random id so we can count a like or an advert once. It is kept in your browser.
- Our servers see your network address when you connect. We use it to limit abuse.
- A photo of a result sheet, if you add one to a result post.
- Verification: if you ask to be verified, a photo or PDF of your government ID and of a utility bill. They are kept privately, are not shown to other people, and only the WAWU team who check them can open them. We keep the decision (who decided, when, and the result). The files are deleted 90 days after the decision, or at once if you delete your account.

## Why we use it
To run VOICES: sign you in, show your posts, count votes once per person, take payments, keep the community safe, answer reports and support messages, choose adverts, and write the weekly report. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

## Adverts
Adverts are chosen by the state on your profile, if you have set one, and by the topic of the question you are looking at. Adverts are not shown on sensitive topics.

## Where it is stored
VOICES runs on DigitalOcean. Voice recordings, photos, share-card images and the videos you ask us to make are kept in DigitalOcean Spaces, in a bucket used only by VOICES. Nightly database copies are kept for 7 days.

## Who else handles it
- Flutterwave handles payments. They tell us whether a payment worked.
- Google Gemini turns voice recordings into transcripts, helps draft the weekly report from posts, and corrects your text when you use Fix my text. For Fix my text, only the text you ask to have fixed is sent, to be corrected. It is used for nothing else. We keep the corrected text for up to 10 minutes only, so that a retried request is not charged twice, and then delete it. It is also deleted if you delete your account. For the weekly report, handles and phone numbers are removed from the text before it is sent, and the draft is told never to name or quote a person.
- WAWU ID, run by WAWU, signs you in.
- We may share information with the authorities where the law requires it.

## Result sheet photos
A photo of a result sheet is never public. Only the WAWU team can see it, to check the numbers. The post itself shows totals only. When your account is deleted, the photo is deleted.

## How long we keep it
- Your account data and posts stay while your account is open. A post you delete disappears from the app, and its text is blanked when your account is deleted.
- When you delete your account we keep it for 14 days. Then every post and reply is blanked, including ones you deleted yourself. Your profile details, likes, reactions, saves, the record of which voices you played, blocks, notifications, settings and invite links (the one you sent and the one that brought you) are deleted. Your link is removed from support messages and from share, advert and usage events. Transcripts are wiped. Unspent tokens are lost.
- ID and utility bill files are deleted 90 days after the team decides, and at once when your account is deleted.
- Recordings, profile photos, result sheet photos, data download files, share images and videos you asked us to make are deleted from storage by a regular clean-up. A video is deleted when its answer is deleted or removed, and when your account is deleted. Uploads that were never finished are deleted after 24 hours.
- Some records stay without your name on them: votes, which keep the one-way code made from your phone number so that one phone votes once; result totals, which stay as a public record; payment records, so that our accounts add up; and moderation records. We keep payment records for seven years, as financial and tax rules require.
- Nightly database copies are kept for 7 days.

## Cookies and local storage
VOICES does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It keeps these things in your browser, and clearing your browser data removes them:
- your signed-in session;
- your light or dark theme choice;
- your guest id;
- a random visitor id, kept as a first-party cookie for up to a year, so a voice you play counts once for you when you are not signed in;
- your sign-up draft, so you can finish joining later;
- drafts of text and voice posts you have not sent, including recordings saved on your device;
- payments you have started, so we can check them if you come back;
- the page to return to after you sign in;
- the code from an invite link you opened, for 30 days, so the person who invited you can see that you answered. Only the code is kept;
- your “Share my word” choice, if you make it before you sign in, until you do;
- a note that you have already opened a share link, so it is counted once.

## Your rights
Subject to the law, you can ask to see your information, correct it, delete it, object to some uses, or complain to the data protection authority. You can export your data and delete your account in Settings. For anything else, write to support@wawuafrica.com.

## Changes
If we change this notice, the new text replaces this one on this page.
